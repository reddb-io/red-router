Settings → Sidebar edits the menu you actually see: areas, entries and their pages, each with a switch, plus the presets. Hiding an entry hides all of its pages, hiding a page removes its tab, and Settings → Sidebar itself cannot be hidden. Saved choices and presets carry over unchanged; the old section/entry ordering and group-label switches are gone because the menu no longer has them.
