Console Log: full screen and an activity sparkline. A strip above the log shows lines per minute for the last 30 minutes, stacked by level (info, warning, error, debug), beside the current rate and the warnings and errors of the last five minutes. Clicking a bar narrows the log to that minute. The log can go full screen with the button or the F key (Esc leaves). `/` jumps to the filter.
