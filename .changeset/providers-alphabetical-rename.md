Providers: every grid is now plain alphabetical order (no provider is promoted to the top), and the "Official Supporter" / "Open Source Friend" badges, the brand-coloured card accents and the affiliate tracking links for Kimi and Cheaper Inference are gone — RedRouter has no supporters. A connection can now be renamed on its own: click the pencil next to its name in the connection list, type, and press Enter (Esc cancels); it no longer depends on the rest of the edit form validating.
